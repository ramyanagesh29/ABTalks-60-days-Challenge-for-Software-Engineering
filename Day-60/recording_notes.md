# Presenter Notes

Suggested length: 4–6 minutes.

1. **Opening (30 seconds):** Introduce yourself and the 60-day challenge.
2. **Journey (45 seconds):** Summarize the topics you practised.
3. **Featured project (90 seconds):** Explain the user problem, stack, architecture, and your contribution.
4. **Challenges (45 seconds):** Share one real bug or concept that took effort to understand.
5. **Lessons (60 seconds):** Discuss testing, complexity, documentation, and communication.
6. **Growth and next steps (45 seconds):** Explain what changed and what you will learn next.

Speak naturally. Do not claim a feature, deployment, metric, or personal milestone unless it is accurate.
