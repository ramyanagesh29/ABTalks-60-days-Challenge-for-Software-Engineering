DAY 31 - DUTCH NATIONAL FLAG

low  = next position for 0
mid  = current element
high = next position for 2

Rules:
0 -> swap with low, low++, mid++
1 -> mid++
2 -> swap with high, high--

Important:
After swapping a 2 with high, do not increment mid because the new
value at mid has not been checked.

Input:
[2, 0, 2, 1, 1, 0]

Output:
[0, 0, 1, 1, 2, 2]
