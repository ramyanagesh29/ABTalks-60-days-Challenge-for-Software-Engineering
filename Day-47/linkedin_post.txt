Day 47/60 🚀 | Treasure Dungeon Collector

💰 Built a treasure optimization system using Dynamic Programming.

💡 Key realization:
The locally best choice is not always globally optimal. By comparing the
“take” and “skip” decisions for every room, I found the maximum treasure
without selecting adjacent rooms.

📈 Optimized the solution to O(N) time and O(1) extra space.
