            |        |
        Flame Burst  Tech Blast

BATTLE FLOW
1. Check whether both heroes have energy.
2. Current attacker invokes attack(opponent).
3. Dynamic dispatch selects the subclass's overridden attack method.
4. Defender loses energy; attacker pays an energy cost.
5. Repeat until one hero's energy reaches zero.
6. Announce the winner.

OOP PRINCIPLES
- Inheritance: FireHero and TechHero reuse Hero.
- Polymorphism: battle() works with any Hero subclass.
- Overriding: each subclass implements a specialized attack.
- Encapsulation: each instance maintains its own energy and power.
